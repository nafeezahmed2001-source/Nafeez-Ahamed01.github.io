From watching planes in Chennai
to building sensors in Zwickau.

Hi, I'm Nafeez. I studied aeronautical engineering in Chennai because I wanted to understand how aircraft really fly. That curiosity never left; it just got wider. Today I work as a research assistant at Fraunhofer IWS in Zwickau while finishing my M.Eng. in Advanced Green Engineering and Sustainable Management at WHZ. Only the thesis is left.

My path here wasn't a straight line. For my bachelor's project I designed a drone landing gear modelled on the way birds grip a branch. In 2023 I moved to Germany to study advanced mechanics at TU Darmstadt, then came to Zwickau, where I joined a cold-plasma lab under Prof. Dr. Fröhlich and helped cut our experiment cycle time by 25%.

At Fraunhofer I work on laser-based leak detection for industrial filters. In practice that means writing Python, testing measurement hardware, and working out why a signal is noisier than it should be. I also bring MATLAB, ANSYS, SolidWorks and R, and a consulting internship taught me to turn 105 dense sustainability documents into reports a manager could actually use. The part I enjoy most is the moment a messy measurement finally makes sense.

I'm looking for a thesis partner now and a full-time role from 2027, in optical sensing, sustainable aviation or R&D. I'm based in Zwickau and happy to move anywhere in Germany. If that sounds like your team, I'd love to hear from you.
